1. Problem Statement: Why Fraud Detection is Broken Today

In modern global commerce, over $35 Trillion in digital transactions occur every year. However, online merchants and financial institutions face a devastating dilemma known as the Fraud-Friction Paradox:

The Industry Challenge Why Traditional Solutions Fail Direct Business Impact
1. High Fraud Losses Card-Not-Present (CNP) fraud, automated credential stuffing, and botnets steal $48 Billion annually. Attackers use headless browsers to test stolen credentials in seconds. Direct chargebacks, processor fines, and lost merchandise.
2. Severe Cart Abandonment To stop fraud, legacy systems force mandatory SMS/OTP codes or 3D-Secure prompts on every single checkout. 15% to 25% cart abandonment. Frustrated good customers give up and leave.
3. Opaque "Black-Box" ML Modern AI models output an arbitrary fraud probability score (e.g., 0.87) with zero justification for why a customer was declined. Customer support cannot help, compliance audits fail under FCRA/GDPR, and merchants don't trust the engine.
4. Slow Processing Latency Asynchronous legacy risk engines take 300ms to 2,000ms to compute risk scores, causing sluggish checkouts. Every 100ms delay drops e-commerce conversion rates by 1%.
💡 The Core Question We Answer:
"How can an online platform stop automated bot attacks and account takeovers in sub-15ms, reward trusted users with frictionless one-click payments, and explain 100% of its decision reasoning transparently?"

2. Why We Built AegisGuard: The Solution & Vision

AegisGuard was engineered from the ground up as a real-time, cloud-native fraud scoring and adaptive step-up authentication microservice. Instead of treating all checkouts with the same heavy-handed hurdles, AegisGuard dynamically measures silent telemetry and behavioural signals in the background.

The Four Architectural Pillars of AegisGuard:

  1. Zero-Friction Behavioral Telemetry: Passively measures how the user interacts with the payment form—mouse movement curvature/jitter entropy, inter-keystroke timing intervals (cadence), dwell time, and clipboard paste detection. Human typing is naturally variable; automated bot scripts type with robotic, zero-variance speed or paste instant strings.
  2. Physical Impossible Travel Calculations (Haversine Formula): Calculates geographic distance and elapsed time between the current transaction and the customer's previous authorized transaction. If a customer transacted in New York 10 minutes ago and suddenly checks out from Miami (1,750 km away), that requires an impossible physical speed of 10,500 km/h (Mach 8.5)—instantly triggering a challenge!
  3. Three-Tier Adaptive Decisions (ALLOW, CHALLENGE, BLOCK):
    • ALLOW (0 – 29): Instant approval in ~10–15ms. 95%+ of trusted customers experience zero hurdles.
    • CHALLENGE (30 – 74): Step-up TOTP verification. Instead of blocking a user on a new device or travel VPN, we allow them to prove their identity safely. Successful verification registers the device as trusted.
    • BLOCK (75 – 100): Automated immediate stoppage of botnets, blacklisted IP ranges, and brute-force attacks.
  4. 100% Explainable Decision Weights: Every decision returns the exact signals that contributed to the score, with normalized weights that add up precisely to the final risk number. No black boxes.

3. Under the Hood: Scoring Engine & Signal Weights

The evaluation pipeline executes in two high-speed stages configured in server/src/config/weights.js:

  • Stage 1 (Pre-Filters): Fast-fail rules checking IP subnet blacklists and hard velocity violations.
  • Stage 2 (Weighted Pipeline): Evaluates travel physics, hardware consistency, behavioral biometrics, and spend anomalies.

🔒 PCI-DSS Security Hygiene: Sensitive 16-digit card numbers (PAN) and CVVs are never stored or logged to disk. The server extracts only the non-sensitive card_last4 and generates a one-way SHA-256 token hash for velocity indexing.

4. Application Architecture & The Two UIs

AegisGuard is divided into two intuitive operational views accessible from the top navigation bar:

🛒 Tab 1: Merchant Checkout Simulator

Simulates an e-commerce checkout page (e.g., luxury electronics store). Captures client telemetry in real time:

  • One-Click Personas: Deterministic presets (Persona A, B, C) for instant demoing.
  • Live Telemetry Widget: Live counter showing form dwell time, mouse jitter entropy, and paste flags.
  • Decision Modal: Shows instantaneous ALLOW, CHALLENGE (with OTP input), or BLOCK with explainable signal breakdowns.
  • Custom Signal Playground: Interactive sliders to modify signals and test any scenario on the fly.

📊 Tab 2: Risk Operations Console

Designed for fraud analysts and security operation teams:

  • Live Server-Sent Events (SSE) Stream: Real-time streaming table showing transactions as they happen.
  • KPI Metric Cards: Approval Rate, Challenge Rate, Block Rate, and sub-15ms Latency.
  • Interactive Recharts: Risk score distribution histogram, top triggered factors, and decision breakdowns.
  • ⚡ Run Attack Simulation: Fires a burst of 6 bot transactions to show live real-time detection.
  • Transaction Inspector Drawer: Side drawer with deep-dive tabs: Factor Weights, Raw Telemetry, and Immutable Audit Trail.

5. How to Run the Application (2-Minute Setup)

Follow these quick commands in your terminal before recording your video:

Step 1: Seed the Database Baseline

npm run seed

This creates 5 diverse user profiles, verified hardware devices, and ~200 historical transactions in the local SQLite database.

Step 2: Start the Development Server

npm run dev

Starts both the Express API (Port 4000) and the React Vite client (Port 3000) concurrently.

Step 3: Open in Chrome

http://localhost:3000

Look at the top navbar: verify that the green "● Live Stream Connected" badge is visible. You're ready to record!

💡 Quick Reset Tip: If you ever make a mistake or want to re-record a scene, simply click the "Reset Demo" button in the top navigation bar. It instantly restores the database to its pristine demo state!

7. Presenter Cheatsheet & Technical Glossary

If viewers or judges ask questions in comments or live Q&A, here are quick 1-sentence answers:

Concept / Term Simple Plain-English Explanation
Haversine Formula A mathematical formula that calculates the great-circle spherical distance between two GPS coordinates on Earth. We divide distance by elapsed time to calculate velocity in km/h.
Mouse Jitter / Entropy Humans move their mouse in natural curves with slight micro-tremors (jitter score > 0.40). Automated scripts use straight-line coordinate jumps or programmatic triggers (jitter ≤ 0.05).
Keystroke Cadence Measures the mean delay and standard deviation between keystrokes. Humans have natural variation (~30–45ms std-dev). Bot scripts inject keys at rigid intervals (std-dev < 15ms) or 0ms.
Canvas / WebGL Fingerprint Renders an invisible graphic in memory using the device's GPU and font engine. Because every GPU and driver renders pixels slightly differently, the resulting hash uniquely identifies hardware without cookies.
Server-Sent Events (SSE) A lightweight, one-way HTTP streaming protocol (GET /api/stream) that pushes real-time transaction updates from the Node.js server directly to the React dashboard with sub-5ms overhead.
PCI-DSS Compliance Sensitive 16-digit card numbers and CVVs are processed strictly in volatile memory. Only the last 4 digits and a one-way cryptographic SHA-256 hash are retained for velocity calculations.
